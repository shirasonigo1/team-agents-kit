# SiteLog field research: working without signal

Brightwell Systems · UX research synthesis · October 2026
Method: 9 contextual interviews with lift and fire-safety inspectors (P1–P9) from 3 customer accounts, plus 2 days of ride-alongs. Each session 60–90 min.

## Who we talked to

| Code | Account | Region | Inspections per week |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1, P2, P3 | Harbourline Lifts | City Central, Metro Underground | 18–25 |
| P4, P5, P6 | Calder Fire Safety | Hospital Estate, Coastal South | 12–16 |
| P7, P8, P9 | Morrow Estates | Rural North | 8–11 |

## Key findings

**F1. Dead zones are routine, not rare.** 7 of 9 inspectors lose signal at least once per inspection. The worst places are lift motor rooms, plant rooms, basements and underground stations. Gaps are often long: P4 described a full inspection (about 50 minutes) with no signal at all.
> "The motor room at the general hospital has zero bars. We do fourteen inspections a month in that building." (P4)

**F2. Paper is the workaround, and re-typing is the cost.** When SiteLog cannot save, inspectors switch to paper checklists and re-type the report on a desktop later. Self-reported re-entry time: 25–55 minutes per day, median 38 minutes.
> "I fill it in twice. Once on paper in the basement, once at night at the kitchen table." (P8)

**F3. Photos are the heavy part.** A typical inspection captures a median of 23 photos (about 2.9 MB each, so roughly 67 MB per inspection). Failed photo uploads are the most-cited frustration (6 of 9).
> "It spins, then it says 'upload failed', and I don't know which photos made it." (P2)

**F4. Signatures must happen on site.** Harbourline Lifts inspectors must hand the site manager a signed report before leaving the site (contract clause 7.2). Offline, they currently print nothing and get a paper signature, which the auditors at Harbourline query every quarter.
> "If I can't get the signature on the tablet, I'm technically in breach." (P1)

**F5. Conflicting edits are rare but scary.** 2 of 9 had seen two inspectors edit the same site report on the same day (handover between shifts). Both lost notes. Nobody wanted a complex merge screen: "Just show me both and let me pick" (P6).

**F6. Trust depends on visible sync state.** Every participant asked for a clear "saved on this device / sent to office" indicator per report and per photo.

## What inspectors need from offline mode (ranked by mentions)

1. Complete a full checklist with no signal and never lose data (9/9)
2. Photos queue reliably and show per-photo status (8/9)
3. Capture the customer signature offline (5/9, all Harbourline and Calder)
4. Clear sync status per report (9/9)
5. Simple handling when two people edited the same report (2/9)

## Out of what we heard (not requested)

- Nobody asked for offline access to the web dashboard; office staff are always online.
- Nobody asked for offline report history older than 30 days.

## Caveats

Small sample (9). Rural North participants all come from one account (Morrow Estates). We did not test any prototype; findings describe current behaviour only.
