TALLYFOX - RECRUITMENT AND ENROLMENT NOTE FOR THE ONBOARDING CHECKLIST EXPERIMENT
UX Research Operations, 21 November 2026

1. WHO CAN BE ENROLLED
- New signups on iOS or Android only. The checklist is built for the mobile apps; there is no web version.
- Must have a verified birth year showing age 18 or over (policy rule R1). About 86 percent of new mobile signups qualify.
- Must have the "Help improve Tallyfox" research setting switched on (policy rule R2). About 50 percent of adult signups opt in.
- Must not be in another onboarding experiment (policy rule R9).

2. HOW MANY PEOPLE WE CAN EXPECT
- New mobile signups: about 1,000 per week (see baseline-cohorts.csv, 16 weeks from 27 July 2026).
- Eligible after age and opt-in filters: about 430 per week.
- Assignment is at signup, 50/50, by a hash of the pseudonymous user id.

3. THE ENROLMENT WINDOW IS FIXED
- Earliest start: Monday 18 January 2027 (after the ethics board meeting on 14 January and the app release of 15 January).
- Latest enrolment day: Sunday 14 February 2027. Tallyfox changes its prices on 1 March 2027, and every enrolled user must finish the day 7-13 retention window before the price change is announced in-app on 28 February.
- That gives 4 weeks of enrolment: roughly 4 x 430 = 1,720 eligible users in total, about 860 per group.

4. NO PANEL TOP-UP
- In-product experiments cannot use paid panels or incentives (policy rule R8), so we cannot buy extra participants.
- Marketing has no campaign planned for January-February, so signups will not rise.

5. NOT DECIDED
- Web signups (about 180 eligible per week) are not covered: the checklist does not exist on web and nobody has decided whether a web version should be built for this study. Raise this with the product director.
